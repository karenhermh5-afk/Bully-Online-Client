Summary:
This is the most basic way of connecting to the Bully Online server via Steam Launch Options. We recommend that you use this if both the Bully Online Launcher and Direct Connect don't work.

How to use:
1. Open your Steam library.
2. Right click on Bully: Scholarship Edition and select "Properties..."
3. Under "LAUNCH OPTIONS" enter the following: --joinServerASAP swegta.com --username YOURUSERNAME
4. Change "YOURUSERNAME" to your swegta.com username.
